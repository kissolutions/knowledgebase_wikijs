## 0. Purpose and Scope

**What this device is:**
A KMC Controls BACnet application-specific controller (KMC "Conquest"-family BAS field controller), used to execute unitary or terminal-equipment control logic.

**Why this device exists:**
Provides programmable I/O and BACnet communication for a single piece of equipment or a small system, as an alternative to a PLC or a larger multi-equipment controller.

**What this page covers:**
- Physical and functional characteristics
- I/O capacity and expansion capability
- Constraints this hardware imposes on controller/panel assignment (bas-logic-gen Stage 8)

**What this page does NOT do:**
- Recommend when to select this controller over another KMC model (see the product-class page in bas-logic-gen)
- Define KIS naming or programming conventions (see `platforms/kmc/` in bas-logic-gen)
